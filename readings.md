# Readings

All short. Read them before the week they're listed under — they're
assigned the week before, and each one is discussed in class. Copies or
page scans are handed out in class or linked from the week's page.

## For week 2
- Gaston Bachelard, *The Poetics of Space*, ch. 1, "The House. From
  Cellar to Garret" — the first ten pages.
- Ivan Sutherland, "The Ultimate Display" (1965) — two pages. Read the
  last paragraph twice.

## For week 3
- Jorge Luis Borges, "The Garden of Forking Paths".

## For week 4

## For week 5

## For week 6

## For week 8

## For week 9

  week 8 page).
