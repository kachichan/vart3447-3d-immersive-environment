# Final project
## Only Works in This Room

**Make a piece for the Immersive Lab.** Any format: projection, video, drawing, light, sound, a performance, objects in the room, people's movement, the 2D or the 3D template. Teams of **3–4**, the same team from week 9 to the end.

**Two rules:**

1. **It only works in this room.** Four walls, a floor, no ceiling, a door in the left wall, an audience that walks around. Use that. If it would work just as well on a laptop screen, it isn't finished.
2. **It runs without you.** On presentation day it has to start cold and keep going while you stand back. A piece that needs perfect tracking, or needs you to fix it, doesn't survive its own opening.

## Bring to your tutorial

1. **Your piece in one sentence.** What happens, and to whom.
2. **A floor plan.** Where things are on the five surfaces, where the audience stands, where they come in.
3. **Something running in the room.** Rough is fine. Ideas on paper don't count.
4. **One question** you can't answer without the room.

The tutorial happens in the room, so test, don't present.

---

## On presentation day

- Your piece runs in the lab. You talk beside it, briefly. **Running order and timing will be posted in week 12.**
- Arrive with everything in one folder (see [Before you come](immersive-lab/#visit)), on a USB drive.
- It starts cold, at your slot. No setting up during someone else's.

## What you submit (by Sunday 6 December, 23:59, on Moodle)

- **A video, 3 minutes at most**, filmed in the lab. Show people in the room: it's a room piece.
- **An artist statement**, 150–200 words, one per team: what it is, what the room does that a screen couldn't, what you want people to leave with.
- **Who did what**: one line per team member.
- **Credits** for anything you didn't make.

One submission per team.

## Grading

| Weight | What |
|---|---|
| **30%** | **Concept**: is there an idea, and does the room carry it? |
| **30%** | **Space and the body**: does it use the walls, the floor, the corners, the door, the audience moving? |
| **20%** | **Technical and reliability**: it works, cold, without you |
| **20%** | **Presentation and documentation**: the talk, the video, the statement |

You must be able to explain everything in your piece, including any code from Gemini.
