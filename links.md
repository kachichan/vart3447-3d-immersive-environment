# Links

Everything you'll be asked to type or open, in one place.

## Moodle

**Lecture slides and reading PDFs** live on the course Moodle site,
filed by week. Find the course in your Moodle course list and sign in
with your HKBU account. Everything else — handouts, rules, links —
is here on this site, no login needed.

## Every week

- **The class board:** [padlet.com/chankachi/vart3447](https://padlet.com/chankachi/vart3447) — one
  column per week. Every week's link (weeks 1–6) or video (weeks 8–12)
  goes in that week's column, with `Week NN — Your Name` as the subject.
- **Your PlayCanvas account page** — lists every project you've made.
  That's your Part A portfolio.
